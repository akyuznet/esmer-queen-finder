## Full description (4000 characters max)

Finding the queen is the slowest part of a hive inspection. ESMER Queen Finder watches the frame through your phone's camera and draws a box around the queen the moment it sees her, then vibrates and beeps so you can keep both hands on the frame.

Why beekeepers like it:

- Free, with no subscription, no account and no ads.
- Works offline in the apiary. Nothing leaves your phone.
- Live detection at around 12 frames per second on a modern phone.
- Drone boxes shown separately so drones are not mistaken for the queen.
- Pinch to zoom for far corners of the frame.
- Adjustable sensitivity and a frames-before-alert setting to suit your hives.
- Snapshot button saves a photo of the frame to your gallery.

How it works: a small neural network trained on thousands of annotated hive photos runs on your phone's GPU. A queen must be seen in several consecutive frames before the app alerts, which keeps false alarms down.

Tips: good light helps. Hold the frame so the bees fill the picture, use the torch under a roof, and lower the sensitivity if you get false alarms on drones.

ESMER Queen Finder is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. The code, the training scripts and the model are at https://github.com/akyuznet/esmer-queen-finder.
