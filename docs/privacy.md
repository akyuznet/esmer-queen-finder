# Privacy Policy for ESMER QUEEN FINDER

Last updated: 9 October 2026

ESMER QUEEN FINDER ("the app") helps beekeepers find the queen bee by analysing the phone camera picture on the device itself.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. It does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only to show a live preview and to run a bee-detection model on the phone. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not saved or sent anywhere, unless you press the Snapshot button, which saves a photo to your own device's Pictures folder under your control.

## Permissions

- Camera: required for the live preview and detection.
- Vibration: used for the alert when a queen is found.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts or storage permissions.

## Third parties

No third-party services receive data from the app. The detection model runs locally using the LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite) runtime bundled in the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date above will be updated.
